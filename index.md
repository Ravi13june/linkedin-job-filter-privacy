# Privacy Policy for LinkedIn Job Filter

**Effective Date:** November 19, 2026  
**Contact:** gehalotkishan@gmail.com

## 1. Overview
LinkedIn Job Filter ("the Extension") is built with a strict privacy-first architecture. All job-filtering operations execute 100% locally within your web browser. We do not operate backend tracking servers, we do not sell user data, and we do not collect your LinkedIn credentials, profile information, or browsing history.

## 2. Information We Process
* **Local Filter Preferences:** Your toggle settings (e.g., Hide Promoted Jobs, Hide Reposted Jobs, and custom blocked company keywords) are stored locally on your device using Chrome's `chrome.storage.local` API. This data never leaves your browser.
* **Pro License Verification Data:** If you choose to purchase a Pro license, you enter your license key into the Extension popup. The Extension sends only your license key and our product identifier via HTTPS to Gumroad (`https://api.gumroad.com/v2/licenses/verify`) to confirm purchase validity and prevent refunded or multi-device abuse. Your license status is then cached locally in `chrome.storage.local`.

## 3. Data We Do NOT Collect
* We do **not** read, store, or transmit your LinkedIn login session, cookies, messages, connections, or job application history.
* We do **not** use third-party analytics trackers, advertising pixels, or telemetry SDKs inside the Extension.

## 4. Third-Party Services
* **Gumroad:** Payment processing and license key issuance are handled by Gumroad, Inc. When you purchase a Pro key on Gumroad, your payment details and email address are governed by [Gumroad's Privacy Policy](https://gumroad.com/privacy).

## 5. Chrome Web Store Limited Use Compliance
The use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/user-data-faq), including the Limited Use requirements. Data accessed by the Extension is used solely to provide and maintain the user-facing job filtering features described in our store listing.

## 6. Data Deletion
You can delete all locally stored preferences and license data at any time by clearing the Extension's storage or uninstalling LinkedIn Job Filter from `chrome://extensions`.

## 7. Changes to This Policy
If our data handling practices ever change, we will update this page with a new effective date and provide prominent notice within the Extension prior to collecting any new category of data.

## 8. Contact
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your Pro license, contact us at **gehalotkishan@gmail.com**.
